Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20th July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you purchase or participate in The Design Session 04: The Hire-Ready Portfolio ("the Course"), operated by Satyam Dubey, doing business as @thedesignshop.tds ("we," "us," "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal details you share with us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you when you register and pay for the Course. This includes:

  • Personal details: Your full name and email address.
  • Contact number: Your WhatsApp number (required to invite you to the cohort WhatsApp group).
  • Payment metadata: Transaction identifiers and payment confirmation from Razorpay (we do not store or see credit card or bank details).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To process your enrollment and confirm your payment.
  • To send you automated confirmation emails and session notifications via SMTP email service.
  • To add you to the course WhatsApp group, which acts as the communications hub for session links, assignments, and updates.
  • To review your portfolio if you submit it for feedback or referral consideration.

3. Payment Security & Data Sharing

All online payments are securely processed through Razorpay. We do not store, access, or collect your financial data, account numbers, or cards on our servers. Razorpay uses industry-standard encryption to protect your financial transactions.

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We share data only with third-party service providers (such as Razorpay and SMTP email servers) strictly to perform the operations required for the Course.

4. Local Storage

Our website uses local storage (stored on your browser) to persist details about any applied early-bird discount coupon codes, ensuring that correct discounted pricing is shown across page visits. This does not contain personal tracking data.

5. Data Security

We take standard technical and administrative steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. However, no digital transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee complete data safety.

6. Access and Correction

If you wish to update, modify, or delete the personal details you provided during registration, please contact us at the address below. We will address your request within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India. Any disputes or issues related to data protection under this policy will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Indore, Madhya Pradesh.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out to us at: hello@thedesignshop.studio
